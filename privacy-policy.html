Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2025

Welcome to ChoreCraft! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our household chore management application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address - for account authentication and communication
  • Display name - to identify you within your household
  • Profile picture (optional) - if you choose to upload one
  • Password - stored securely using industry-standard encryption

1.2 Household Data

To provide our core functionality, we collect and store:

  • Household information - household name, invite codes, settings
  • Chore data - chore titles, descriptions, assignments, completion status, photos
  • Reward data - reward titles, descriptions, coin values, redemption history
  • Activity logs - records of chores completed, rewards claimed, and coins earned
  • Member roles - household owner, admin, or member status

1.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information about your device and app usage:

  • Device information - device type, operating system version
  • App usage - features used, frequency of use, performance data
  • Push notification tokens - to send you notifications about chores and rewards
  • Crash reports - to identify and fix technical issues

1.4 Photos

If you choose to upload photos for chores (before/after photos), these are stored securely in our cloud storage. You can delete these photos at any time by deleting the associated chore.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve ChoreCraft's services:

2.1 Core Functionality

  • Create and manage your household account
  • Allow you to create, assign, and complete chores
  • Track coin earnings and reward redemptions
  • Send notifications about chores, rewards, and household activities
  • Enable collaboration within your household

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze app usage to improve features and user experience
  • Identify and fix bugs and technical issues
  • Develop new features based on user needs

2.3 Communication

  • Send important service updates and security alerts
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Send push notifications (which you can disable in settings)

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Where We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Services (Google Cloud Platform), with servers located in the United States. Firebase is a industry-leading platform that provides enterprise-grade security and reliability.

3.2 How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption - All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
  • Authentication - Secure password-based authentication with optional biometric login (Face ID/Touch ID)
  • Access Control - Strict database security rules ensure users can only access their own household data
  • Regular Backups - Automated backups to prevent data loss
  • Secure Infrastructure - Hosted on Google Cloud Platform with 99.9% uptime SLA

3.3 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4.1 Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate ChoreCraft:

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, cloud storage, and hosting
  • RevenueCat - Subscription and payment processing (no payment card data is stored by us)
  • Expo - Push notifications and app infrastructure

These providers are bound by strict data protection agreements and only process data on our behalf.

4.2 Within Your Household

Data you create in ChoreCraft (chores, rewards, activities) is shared with other members of your household. This is essential for the app's core functionality.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights, but only to the minimum extent necessary.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Export

You can access all your data within the app. To request a copy of your data for export, contact us at the email address below.

5.2 Correction and Deletion

  • Update information - Edit your profile, chores, and rewards directly in the app
  • Delete specific data - Delete chores, rewards, or photos individually
  • Delete account - Request full account deletion by contacting support

5.3 Notification Preferences

You can control push notifications in the app's Settings screen. You can enable/disable notifications by category and set quiet hours.

5.4 Biometric Data

If you enable Face ID or Touch ID, your biometric data is stored securely on your device only. We never receive or store your biometric data on our servers. You can disable biometric authentication at any time in Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

ChoreCraft is designed for use by families and may be used by children under the age of 13 as part of a family household. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Parental Control - We provide parental controls allowing adults to mark household members as children
  • Limited Data Collection - We only collect information necessary for app functionality
  • No Third-Party Advertising - We do not show ads or share children's data with advertisers
  • Parental Access - Parents can review and delete their child's data by managing the household account

Parent/Guardian Consent: By creating a household account and adding family members, parents/guardians consent to the collection and use of their children's information as described in this policy.

7. Premium Subscriptions

If you purchase a premium subscription:

  • Payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store
  • We do not store your payment card information
  • We only receive confirmation of your subscription status from the app store
  • Subscription data is stored to unlock premium features for your household

8. International Data Transfers

ChoreCraft is operated from the United Kingdom, but our data is stored on Google Cloud Platform servers in the United States. By using our app, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify you through the app or via email
  • Request your consent if required by law

Your continued use of ChoreCraft after changes are made constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Cookie Policy

ChoreCraft does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, which does not track individual users across websites or apps.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know - Request information about data we collect and how we use it
  • Right to Delete - Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out - We don't sell personal information, so no opt-out is needed
  • Right to Non-Discrimination - We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

12. European Union (GDPR) Rights

If you are located in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

  • Right to Access - Obtain confirmation of what personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification - Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure - Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to Restrict Processing - Request limitation on how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability - Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object - Object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Withdraw Consent - Withdraw previously given consent at any time

Legal Basis: We process your data based on:

  • Contract Performance - To provide ChoreCraft services to you
  • Consent - Where you have given explicit consent
  • Legitimate Interest - To improve our services and prevent fraud
